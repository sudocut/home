# Voice

> **Say it, then stop.**

The product removes footage. The writing removes words.

`DIRECT` · `NUMERIC` · `EDITOR TO EDITOR` · `NEVER PRECIOUS` · `SHORTER THAN YOU THINK`

---

## The register

From soul.md: **honesty over polish.** No hype. A demo that looks more capable
than the live product breaks trust — that is the Humane lesson. We show the
failures and the 삽질.

We are talking to an editor who has done this by hand and knows exactly how long
it takes. Respect that. Never explain what they already know. Never sell.

## Korean — 한국어가 기본

Korean is home, English runs in parallel. Korean copy is written *as Korean*, not
translated from English. Keep the same register: numeric, direct, editor to editor.

**이렇게 씁니다**

- "4시간 촬영. 한 편의 이야기. 작업은 90초."
- "6분 12초 잘라냈습니다. 필요한 장면은 그대로예요."
- "더 짧게 자르세요."
- "손으로 자른 컷과 AI가 자른 컷을 나란히 올렸습니다. 아무도 구분하지 못했어요."
- "AI가 제안하고, 결정은 당신이 합니다."

**이렇게 쓰지 않습니다**

- ~~"AI로 크리에이티브 워크플로우를 혁신하세요."~~
- ~~"당신의 콘텐츠 잠재력을 완벽하게 실현합니다."~~
- ~~"놀랍도록 매끄러운, 매끄럽도록 놀라운."~~
- ~~"최첨단 AI 기술을 활용한 차세대 편집 솔루션."~~

Notes on Korean register:

- Prefer `-습니다` / `-예요` over `-하십시오`. We are a peer, not a vendor.
- Numbers stay numerals: **6분 12초**, not 육 분 십이 초.
- Avoid 외래어 padding — 솔루션, 플랫폼, 최적화 are usually deletable.
- Product vocabulary already in use (`bauhaus.js`): 침묵 · 군더더기 · 재시도 · 딴길
  for cut reasons, 확인 필요 for low confidence. Reuse it; don't invent synonyms.

## Numbers are the argument

The strongest thing we have is evidence, so lead with it.

- Say **6:12**, not "significant time savings."
- Say **7 scenes**, not "several segments."
- Say **14:32 → 10:47**, not "dramatically shorter."

If a sentence has no number in it, ask whether it needs to exist.
