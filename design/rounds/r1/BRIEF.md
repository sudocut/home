# r1 — brief

> Appended to `design/BRIEF.md`, which carries the brand, the rules, the output
> contract, and the rubric. This file says only what is different about this round.

## What to design

**The homepage above the fold** — everything a first-time visitor sees before
scrolling, at 1280×800.

Not the whole page. One screen.

## What this round is testing

**What has to be on the first screen for a stranger to understand SudoCut and
believe it?**

Specifically: does the *proof* (the blind A/B test where viewers couldn't tell the
hand-edited cut from the AI-edited one) belong above the fold, or does it dilute
the promise? Variants may answer differently — that is the point.

## Must include

1. **The positioning line.** `sudo rm the boring parts.` — or a Korean line that
   carries the same idea. Your call how prominent.
2. **What we do, in one sentence a non-technical creator understands.** Remember
   the main audience is Korean video creators, not developers.
3. **Exactly one call to action.** One. In cobalt. Decide what it should be —
   waitlist, demo, contact — and make that decision legible.
4. **The wordmark or mark.** `/brand/logo/mark.svg`, `lockup-horizontal.svg`, or
   `lockup-stacked.svg` — reference by absolute path, they are served.

## Explicitly out of scope

- No pricing, no feature grid, no team section, no footer nav, no testimonials.
- No second screen. If it needs scrolling, it is out of scope for r1.
- No product screenshots — we are not shipping fake UI. Honesty over polish.
- No signup form fields. One button is enough.

## Notes

- Korean is primary. English may run in parallel, but a Korean visitor must never
  feel like they're reading a translation.
- The blind A/B proof, if you use it: we shipped a hand-edited cut and an
  AI-edited cut side by side and viewers couldn't tell the difference. Numbers
  beat adjectives — `14:32 → 10:47`, `6:12 trimmed`, `7 scenes`.
- Remember `restraint` is a scored criterion. A screen with four things on it will
  usually beat one with nine.
