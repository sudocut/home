About

The chore is real. So we remove it.

Every long-form creator spends hours cutting dead air by hand. Nobody’s creativity lives there. That is the whole reason SudoCut exists.

The chore

Fillers. Fumbles. Silences. Tangents. NG takes.

Every upload carries hours of them, and every one is cut by hand. Slow, repetitive, and the same on every project. So we sudo rm it: AI strips the boring 80%.

The last 20% is where the creativity lives. Story, timing, emphasis, final call. We don’t touch it. It stays with the creator.

A/B

The evidence · blind A/B

Viewers couldn’t tell the difference.

We shipped a hand-edited cut and an AI-edited cut side by side.

That evidence is why SudoCut is a product and not a pitch.

The handoff

AI proposes. You decide.

The cut we hand back is a proposal, not a verdict. SudoCut exports editor-ready FCPXML and SRT, and you finish in your own tools. Your timeline, your taste, your call.

exports · .fcpxml · .srt

The line

Not a suite. Not a machine.

We won’t build a do-everything editing suite, and we won’t build a one-button machine that ships a finished film with nobody in the loop. We remove a chore. That is the product.
