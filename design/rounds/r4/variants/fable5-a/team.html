Team

How the work gets decided.

No names on this page yet; the roster isn’t cleared for publication. What is cleared: the four rules the work runs on.

01

AI proposes, humans decide.

The model drafts the cut; the creator makes the call. The same rule runs the company. Nothing ships on a model’s say-so.

02

Direction over perfection.

A rough cut in the right direction beats a polished cut in the wrong one. We ship the first and correct in the open.

03

Honesty over polish.

A stub is labelled a stub. A reconstruction is labelled a reconstruction. The empty roster below is this rule, applied.

04

We are not artists.

Work is judged by whether it works, not by how it feels to us. This site’s own layout was chosen by blind ranking, not taste.
