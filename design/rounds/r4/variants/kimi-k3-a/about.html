WHY WE EXIST

Editing is the work. Scrubbing dead air isn't.

Every long-form creator does the same low-value chore by hand: cutting fillers, fumbles, silences, tangents, NG takes. It's slow, repetitive, and nobody's creativity lives there. So we sudo rm it.

01

The chore is universal.

Solo YouTubers, podcast and interview editors, lecture makers, talk-heavy streamers. Everyone with raw footage pays the same hours before the real edit can start. That payment is not craft. It's tax.

02

The last 20% is the work.

AI strips the boring 80%. Pacing, tone, and the final call stay with the creator, because that's where the creativity lives. AI proposes; humans decide. Every cut we make is a suggestion, never a shipment.

03

Your tools finish it.

We export editor-ready FCPXML and SRT, so the cut lands in the editor you already use. No new timeline to learn, no lock-in. Less footage. More story.

A/B

Viewers couldn't tell the difference.

We shipped the same footage cut twice — once by a human editor, once by SudoCut — and put the two versions in front of viewers side by side. Nobody could call which was which. That evidence is why SudoCut is a product, not a pitch.
