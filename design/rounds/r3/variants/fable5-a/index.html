How it works

AI proposes. You decide.

  1. 01

    Drop in the raw footage.

    Hours of talking head, podcast, interview, lecture, or stream. If it’s spoken video, it’s in scope.

  2. 02

    It marks the boring 80%.

    Fumbles, retakes, tangents, dead air. Every proposed cut is timecoded, and nothing is deleted until you say so.

  3. 03

    You keep the final say.

    Approve the cut, export FCPXML + SRT, and finish in the editor you already use. Say the raw ran 14:32. You review 10:47. Nothing you wanted is gone.

Who it’s for

Not for

  • Anyone shopping for a do-everything editing suite.
  • Anyone who wants a finished film with no human in it.
